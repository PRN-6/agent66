# 👥 Character Lore, Visual Design & Mechanics

Agent66 features **6 core characters**. Any character can be directly possessed and controlled by the player via the Top Roster Bar or dialogue prompt, while the other 5 live in the world as interactive NPCs with custom dialogue and behaviors.

## 🔄 Dynamic Character Switching Flow

When the player clicks any portrait or selects **"Play as this Character ⚡"**:
```
User Action (Click Roster / Dialogue)
         │
         ▼
`switchPlayableCharacter(charId, teleportToSpawn)`
         │
         ├── 1. If currently mounted, safely dismount steed
         ├── 2. `player.setCharacter(charId)`: Swaps procedural 3D model in Player group
         ├── 3. `npcManager.updateActiveCharacter(charId)`: Re-spawns previous character as NPC, removes newly chosen character from NPC pool
         ├── 4. `hud.setActiveCharacter(charId)`: Highlights the active avatar on the UI
         └── 5. Teleports player to character's home location (if triggered from dialogue)
```

## ⏳ 9. Racial Lifespans, Aging Rates & Time Philosophies

In this medieval realm, time is perceived fundamentally differently by each race. Lifespans range from the fleeting mortal embers of humans to the eternal, ageless void entities of demons.

| Race | Canonical Lifespan | Aging Speed | Homeland Territory | Cultural Trait | Philosophy of Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Humans (`human`)** | **75 – 100 Years** | `1.0x` (Mortal Baseline) | Kingdom of Valoria (North-East) | Rapid Ambition & Legacy Builders | *“Life is a fierce flame that burns bright and short. In fewer than a hundred winters, we build kingdoms, write treaties, and defy the eternal dark.”* |
| **Dwarves (`dwarf`)** | **350 – 500 Years** | `0.2x` (Stone Longevity) | Karak-Drak Bastion (South-East) | Unbreakable Unity & Generational Craft | *“A human counts in seasons; a dwarf counts in mountain strata. Stone endures, brotherhood outlasts empires, and what is forged united stands forever.”* |
| **Elves (`elf`)** | **1,500 – 3,000+ Years** | `0.03x` (Celestial Starlight) | Sylvaheim Canopy (South-West) | Perpetual Youth & Cosmic Harmony | *“We watch mortal dynasties blossom and wither like morning lilies. Time is not a race to run, but an ancient melody to harmonize with.”* |
| **Demons (`demon`)** | **Ageless / Eternal (10,000+ Yrs)** | `0.00x` (Void Cycle Rebirth) | Obsidian Wastes (North-West) | Immortal Vessels & Magma Rebirth | *“Mortals cling to fleeting heartbeats like drowning insects. We were here before your mountains cooled, and our obsidian flames will burn long after your castles turn to dust.”* |

### 🧬 Biological & Physical Aging Profiles

1. **Humans of Valoria (`75 – 100 Years`)**:
   - **Aging Cycle**: Fast-paced mortal development. Peak physical condition from age 18 to 35. Distinguished silvering and veteran wisdom emerge in the 50s. Revered village matrons and court elders thrive into their 70s and 80s.
   - **Psychological Driver**: Aware of their brief window, humans build stone monuments, write laws, and rush into action with immense urgency.

2. **Dwarves of Karak-Drak (`350 – 500 Years`)**:
   - **Aging Cycle**: Physical prime lasts well past 150 years. Dense bone density, stone-hard resilience, and heavy braided beards that only turn iron-silver after 300 winters.
   - **Psychological Driver**: Generational patience. An architectural project or masterwork blade can take 40 years to craft. Unity and clan brotherhood are prized above all.

3. **Elves of Sylvaheim (`1,500 – 3,000+ Years`)**:
   - **Aging Cycle**: Sustained by starlight communion with the Colossal Elder Trees and Celestial Moon Orb. Elves maintain perpetual youthful beauty across millennia; aging manifests not as physical decay, but as luminous eyes and profound cosmic tranquility.
   - **Psychological Driver**: Contemplative and serene. Wars between mortal realms seem like momentary squabbles across their multi-millennium memories.

4. **Demons of the Nether Crags (`Ageless / Eternal`)**:
   - **Aging Cycle**: Immune to biological senescence. Their obsidian and magma vessels endure for 10,000+ years. If their physical body is slain in battle, their nether core re-condenses over centuries within boiling magma fissures.
   - **Psychological Driver**: Primal dominion. They view other races as fragile, temporary intruders upon an ancient volcanic continent.

## ⚔️ 10. Inter-Racial Friction, Animosity & Cultural Stereotypes

In this realm, the 4 races actively dislike each other and hold entrenched stereotypes and mutual grievances:

### 1. 🔥 Demons ("Demons Don't Like Anyone")
- **Core Disposition**: Universal hatred, misanthropy, and unyielding aggression. They consider all mortal life to be insectoid, fragile cattle awaiting the obsidian pyre.
- **Racial Stance**:
  - **vs. Humans**: Despises their weak, squishy bodies and stone castles. Views them as kindling for the Nether Forge.
  - **vs. Elves**: Despises their delicate white robes and narcissistic "starlight nobility." Vows to burn the Colossal Elder Trees into cooking ash.
  - **vs. Dwarves**: Despises their tunnel-burrowing and mechanical toys. Promises their mountains will become their magma tombs.
  - **Diplomacy**: Non-existent. Demons do not form treaties; they only conquer, consume, and burn.

### 2. 🌙 Elves ("Elves Love to Be Noble")
- **Core Disposition**: Aristocratic vanity, supreme high nobility, and celestial poise. They view themselves as the firstborn cosmic aristocracy and look down their noses at all other beings.
- **Racial Stance**:
  - **vs. Demons**: Abhorrent abominations devoid of grace, culture, or honor that must be eradicated with starlight.
  - **vs. Dwarves**: Repulsed by their soot-covered skin, greasy wrenches, loud mechanical clatter, and total lack of artistic poetry.
  - **vs. Humans**: Condescending tolerance. Views humans as loud, frantic children whose seventy-year lives fly by before they ever achieve true elegance.
  - **Cultural Habit**: Obsessed with immaculate white marble, celestial circlets, silk robes, and moon poetry.

### 3. 🛡️ Dwarves ("Dwarves Are Good with Technology")
- **Core Disposition**: Mechanical genius, industrial engineering supremacy, and clan brotherhood. Proud innovators who harness steam boilers, giant rotating cog gears, and automated rail transit.
- **Racial Stance**:
  - **vs. Demons**: Overgrown torches asking for a pneumatic forge hammer to the jaw.
  - **vs. Elves**: Laughs at their prancing vanity and flower-singing. Boasts that a single 20-ton iron gear has more utility than three millennia of elven poetry.
  - **vs. Humans**: Considers humans flimsy builders whose wooden homes rot in decades, but respects their gold and trade caravans.
  - **Technological Marvels**: Great twin blast furnaces, rotating 8-tooth mountain gears, pneumatic lifts, and minecart rail networks.

### 4. 👑 Humans ("Humans Are Good with Their Stuff")
- **Core Disposition**: Resourceful adaptability, practical craftsmanship, agricultural abundance, and gritty perseverance. Aware of their short lifespans, humans accomplish more in decades than others do in centuries.
- **Racial Stance**:
  - **vs. Demons**: Terrified yet resolute foes; human knights hold the front lines along the Great River to protect the continent.
  - **vs. Elves**: Resents their aristocratic snobbery and lofty condescension; human merchants proudly remind elves that human grain feeds their border outposts.
  - **vs. Dwarves**: Pragmatic business partners. Humans negotiate hard for dwarven steel and gears, despite dwarven stubbornness.
  - **Human Strengths**: Vast wheat and produce fields (Oakhaven), bustling bazaar commerce, high fortified citadel walls, and versatile diplomacy.
